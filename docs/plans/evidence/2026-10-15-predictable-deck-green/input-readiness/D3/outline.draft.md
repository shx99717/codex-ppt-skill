Speaker notes:

Hi everyone.

Let me show you the access journey we are proposing. Imagine a colleague who needs to open two different providers. This is a simple illustration.

Speaker notes:

So, the colleague moves to the second provider using the same account.

That does not mean the two providers give the colleague the same access. Each provider can have its own permissions.

We also have not settled every sign-in detail. Using the same account alone does not tell us whether another sign-in step will appear. What stays clear is the personal account and the separate provider access.
