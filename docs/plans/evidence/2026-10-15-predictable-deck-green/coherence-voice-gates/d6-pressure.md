## Exact final speaker notes

Let’s imagine Maya for a moment. She’s a made-up colleague, just to help us picture the problem.

She’s trying to open something she needs for work. There are three different portals in our example, and each has its own account. So before she can get to the thing she needs, she has to work out which login belongs to which place.

That’s the awkward bit. The task she came for may be simple, but the first question is about accounts. Am I in the right portal? Is this the account I use here?

Maybe she knows all three logins perfectly well. She still has to pick the right one for this place. That’s what I want us to focus on: the choice before the work.

So, let’s look at how a shared account could make that login choice simpler.
